## The brief

A visualization project: design a wall charger for electric cars as if it were made by Koenigsegg, and present it in photoreal renders.

## The design

The charger is built around a light centre spine with the logo at the top and the controls below it, wrapped by two high-gloss black wings that mirror their surroundings. The Type 2 connector docks at the side, and its handle continues the same light-and-black language.

When the car is charging, a thin light line along the wings and the logo change colour, so the charging state can be read from across the driveway.

## The renders

The series places the charger on a stone wall at dusk, with warm reflections in the black wings. It moves from the full front view to side views that show how slim the body is, and on to close-ups of the controls and the connector.
