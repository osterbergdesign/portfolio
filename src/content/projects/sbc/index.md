## The idea

STATIC is my own made-up brand — it's also printed on the circuit board of my smart blind. For SBC I imagined it as a television network, and explored how a family of channels can be built from one set of parts, the way the big broadcasters run themed channels under one name.

## The parts

**The wordmark.** Geometric letters with rounded ends, and a play triangle set into the B. "Static Broadcasting Channel" sits underneath in widely spaced capitals.

**The pictograms.** Four rounded tiles, one per theme: a tyre, a lab flask, a smoking factory and a gorilla. On their own they work as channel icons; in a row they show the range of the network.

**The sub-brands.** SBC Discovery tops the pictogram row with the Colosseum and a broadcast antenna, and pairs a compact SBC mark with the channel name. The same Colosseum also sits above the main wordmark, so the pieces can be recombined for each channel.
