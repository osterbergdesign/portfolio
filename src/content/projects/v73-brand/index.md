## Scope

V73 AB offers administration and construction-management consulting. The manual sets the rules the company's marketing and brand should follow, so anyone who produces material for them gets the same result.

## What's in it

- **Logo use.** Vertical, horizontal and symbol-only configurations. The descriptor text is optional — a helpful addition, not a requirement.
- **Logo colour.** Three base colourways, each matched to the background it sits on.
- **Colour.** A primary navy (#283880) with an off-white and a near-black, plus a secondary range of oranges, creams and blues for backgrounds, icons and illustrations. Every colour is given in RGB, CMYK and HEX.
- **Type.** Raleway as the headline typeface for recognition, Inter in regular, medium and bold for longer texts.
- **In use.** Email signatures for the company's staff.
