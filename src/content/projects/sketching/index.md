## Ideation

Fast fineliner thumbnails around coffee — grinders, brewers, cups and bean storage — with two directions pulled forward in colour: *The Gambler* and *The Roomba*. The annotations keep the thinking visible: a bean chute, a screen, where the beans go in.

## Materials in marker

The same faceted pillar, drawn five times and rendered with markers so it reads as different materials: cork, glass, black and grey rubber, marble, concrete, anodised aluminium, transparent green and red plastic, leather and green cloth.

## Digital render

A watch, painted digitally in layers. First the base colours, blocked out with fill selections. Then basic 1-2-3 shading to define the shape, with the crown treated as a simple cylinder. Lighting came next: shadows on the edges that needed them, a cast shadow from the case onto the strap, broad highlights on the case and screen, and heavy stroke highlights to suggest brushed aluminium. A texture on the strap keeps it from looking flat.
